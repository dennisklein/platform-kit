etcd works only while a majority of its members are up.
If you approve three reboots on three control planes at once, the operator still takes down one at a time.

## The two checks

Before a step takes a control plane down, the guard checks:

1. **One at a time.** No other control plane of the same cluster is in the middle of such an operation.
2. **A healthy quorum.** etcd is fully healthy without this node. Each other member answers, has no error, knows a leader and is not a learner. etcd has no alarm. The members that stay are a majority.

The guard counts from the etcd member list, not from the TalosNodes. A member that a wiped node left behind still counts against the majority.

When the first step that takes the node down is its own [](act:EtcdLeave), as in a release, the member leaves while the other members answer.
The guard then counts the majority from the smaller cluster, so a release can take two control planes down to one.
The last member still needs the override.

## Terms

<dl>
  <dt>quorum</dt>
  <dd>A majority of the etcd voting members. Three members tolerate one failure. Two members tolerate none.</dd>
  <dt>learner</dt>
  <dd>A new etcd member that copies data and has no vote yet.</dd>
  <dt>Steps that take a node down</dt>
  <dd>A power cycle, a power off, [](act:TalosReboot), [](act:TalosShutdown), [](act:TalosUpgrade), [](act:TalosReset), [](act:EtcdLeave), and [](act:TalosApplyConfig) in mode `Reboot` or `Auto`.</dd>
  <dt>ClusterDisruption</dt>
  <dd>The `blockedOn` reason of an operation that the guard holds. Nothing happened to the machine yet.</dd>
</dl>

## What the guard does not hold

- Workers.
- Control planes that are not etcd members: they still join, or they already left.
- A cluster that was never bootstrapped. On day 0 all control planes install at once.

## Override

<Callout type="caution">
Use `metal.pk.io/override-cluster-guard` only to repair an etcd that is already broken, or for a cluster with one control plane.
It lifts check 2 only. Check 1 and [](concept:self-protection) stay.
</Callout>
